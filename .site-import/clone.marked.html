CORE VALUE - Artists

We deeply recognize the unique, irreplaceable value of human creativity. While AI-generated work is flooding the internet, we are actively pushing for a massive resurgence of original 2D, digital, and traditional art. We are committed to moving away from the homogenous, often flawed look of AI to prove that nothing beats authentic human talent. To make this happen, we are aggressively recruiting talented artists and designers worldwide to join our team. Our primary mission right now is to have our designs recognized globally, driving toward our ultimate goal: becoming the #1 Art Studio, #2 in overall Creations, and #3 in Development worldwide. 



We prioritize original, unique designs and content for you! 

We ensure that when or if AI is to be used we use it within fair and approriate defined grounds!

CORE VALUE - Developers

Stargame Studio is dedicated to transforming ambitious ideas into fully realized digital experiences. Whether you are looking to build an immersive game for Roblox or Steam, develop a mobile app, launch a dynamic website, or code custom Discord bots, we provide the technical expertise and collaborative environment to make it happen. We are committed to supporting both indie innovators and larger organizations in bringing their software and gaming visions to life across any platform.


We welcome any and everybody into our community! Gamers, investors, supporters, developers, designers and more!

We accept PayPal and ROBUX Through Gamepasses!

NOTICES

Asset Ownership & Credits: Any work submitted to Stargame Studio becomes the property and asset of the studio. We may continue to use and display work created by former designers. By submitting your work to us, you agree that legal action cannot be taken regarding pending compensation, provided you are officially on our list to be paid. Additionally, while we value our team's contributions, we reserve the right to include or omit individual credits on our website and projects to keep our layouts clean and manageable.


AI Usage Policy: We do NOT allow fully AI-generated work. AI must be used responsibly and strictly within defined limits—specifically, it may only be used to assist human creators with mundane processes, quick research, or professionalizing your workflow.


Client Payment Security: If we are hired for a commission and have concerns regarding payment details, we reserve the right to request a 50% upfront deposit. This policy is in place to ensure both the commission process and the final payment are secure for everyone involved.


Hiring & Vetting: When we are hiring designers or developers, we reserve the right to request a comprehensive portfolio or assign a small, specific example task to accurately verify your skills before bringing you onto the team.
